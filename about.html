About

Elder Pako Kwabya is a devoted servant of God with a pastoral heart and a prophetic burden for believers to live aligned, fruitful, and impactful lives. His teachings emphasize faith, wisdom, stewardship, and discipline — calling believers not merely to hope for breakthrough, but to walk in the daily obedience that sustains it.

Through years of pastoral ministry and prophetic teaching, Elder Pako has written and taught extensively on the tensions every believer eventually faces — between promise and process, faith and fear, calling and cost. Threshold of Faith exists to carry that same teaching beyond the four walls of a service: sound, Scripture-rooted doctrine for anyone who wants to go deeper than the surface.

What you'll find here

Sermons built on careful reading of Scripture rather than passing feeling — teaching meant to be returned to, studied, and applied. Alongside the sermons, Elder Pako's books explore these same themes at greater length: enduring seasons of uncertainty and betrayal, breaking through personal and spiritual limitation, and pressing in prayer until promises are fully birthed.

Other writings

Elder Pako is also the author of Navigating Uncertainties, Kiss of Betrayal, Breaking Limits and Limitations, and Tenacity of Birthing the Supernatural, alongside 21 Days Prayers for Financial Success. You can find all of his books on the Books page.
